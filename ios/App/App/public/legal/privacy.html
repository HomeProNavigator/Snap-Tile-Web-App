SnapTile Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026.

SnapTile is a puzzle and building game from SnapTile, Henderson, Nevada, United States. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the SnapTile iPhone app.

What stays on your device

The app can store a first name, puzzle progress, badges, sound setting, and saved builds. You do not have to enter a name. This information stays on the device. SnapTile does not receive it, does not sell it, and does not use it for advertising.

What we do not collect

SnapTile does not collect location, contacts, photos, identifiers for advertising, or analytics. The app has no account server and no ads.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Apple receives the payment details. SnapTile does not receive your card number or your Apple ID password. Apple's privacy policy describes how Apple handles that payment.

Children

The game can be played by children with a parent. A child's name, if one is typed in, never leaves the device. We do not knowingly send personal information about a child to SnapTile, because the app does not send that information to us at all.

How to delete it

Open Account and tap Erase this device. That removes the name, progress, and saved builds from that device. Uninstalling the app removes them as well. Because we never receive a copy, there is no server record to delete.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can ask for access, correction, or deletion of personal data a company holds about you. SnapTile does not hold a copy of the name or the builds. You can erase what is on the device yourself. To ask a question, email support@snaptile.app.

Contact

SnapTile, Henderson, Nevada, United States. Email support@snaptile.app.
